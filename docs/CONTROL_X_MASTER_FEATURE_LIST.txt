 Part 1: How the 3-to-4 Second Power Button Activation Works (Even in Debug APK)
Yes, this works 100% in your Debug APK (app-debug.apk).

Android does not care if the app is release or debug. Android allows any installed app that declares VoiceInteractionService and ACTION_ASSIST to become the phone's Default Digital Assistant.

How to configure your phone once the Debug APK is installed:
Open phone Settings ➡️ Apps ➡️ Default Apps ➡️ Digital Assistant App.
Select Control X (instead of Google Assistant).
Go to Settings ➡️ System ➡️ Gestures (or search "Power Button" in Settings search bar):
Select "Press and hold power button" ➡️ set to "Digital Assistant".
Done! Whenever you press and hold the physical power button continuously for 3 to 4 seconds, Android bypasses Google and launches Control X directly over whatever app is on your screen.
